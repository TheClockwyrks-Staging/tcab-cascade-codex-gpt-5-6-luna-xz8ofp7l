# Cascade

Cascade is a calm, tactile game of Draw One Klondike. Deal a fresh table and
work the seven columns down in alternating colors while building four suited
foundations from ACE through KING.

Turn the STOCK one card at a time onto the waste. Move cards and runs with the
pointer, or DOUBLE-CLICK a playable card to send it home. Empty columns are
reserved for a KING, and an exhausted stock recycles for another pass.

The ending is the reward: when the final card reaches a foundation, the table
breaks into motion. Every card launches in sequence, arcs under gravity,
bounces across the floor, and leaves a painted trail behind until the felt is
buried in the aftermath.
